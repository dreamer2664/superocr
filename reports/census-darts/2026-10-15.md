## Closed-lane fingerprint — and the next DIFFERENT mechanism

The fingerprint is **fused connectivity**: dart strikes are long thin ink
runs that bridge adjacent glyphs in the mask; connected components merge;
both segmenters under-box ~0.5/true. Checked against every closed lane:

- **NOT adaptive threshold (S32, closed):** zero empty masks, ink healthy
  3.6–7.8% — binarisation is never the failure; no threshold lane to reopen.
- **NOT CLAHE (S51, closed net-harmful):** contrast adequate on all 40.
- **NOT morph-close (S46, closed):** pieces are FUSED (0.5/true), not
  broken — closing would fuse them harder; the mechanism is the inverse.
- **NOT edge enhancement (S33, closed):** matcher gets solid fused blobs,
  not fuzzy edges (`?` patterns are shapeless, not faint).
- **NOT straight-column / template / CNN-cut splits (S8/S10c, closed) and
  NOT the S56 topology seam (closed, measured NO-GO):** the naive read of
  "boxes fused → split them" is exactly those lanes; S56's curved
  min-edge-cut seam measured held-out net 0/−1 on its own panels and
  **−20 against shipping** (`reports/s56-seam/RESULTS.md`). Untouched.

**Proposed next mechanism (untried, stated for whoever takes S70/S71's
lane):** prevent the fusion at its source instead of splitting it after —
a **dart-artifact eraser between gray and mask**, gated by the family
preclassifier: detect long thin high-aspect-ratio ink runs spanning the
text band (the dart signature), erase + locally inpaint them in GRAY space,
then let the unchanged route binarise/segment/read. It is none of the
closed lanes: not thresholding (otsu values unchanged), not contrast, not
morphology-close (selective subtraction, not dilation), not post-mask
splitting (S56 worked on the mask; this works before it exists). Honest
caveats, measured-never-assumed: dart ink overlaps glyph ink (eraser
leaves holes → needs inpainting discipline), and the preclassifier gate
must not leak the eraser onto other families — both are preregisterable
probe controls, not reasons to skip.
