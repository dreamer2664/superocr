## Mechanism classes (ranked by expected lift)

1. **Post-match rules breaking correct native reads — 3 confirmed
   instances.** (a) easy-0068 `7`→`g`: the ld-context letter-swap
   (baseline `_LD_CTX_ON`) fires on a 0.044 margin and replaces a
   correct rank-1 digit. (b) easy-0068 `l`→`L`: `_narrow_case_resolve`
   overrides a 0.9946 lowercase match because the word looks all-caps.
   (c) strippy-0005 `g`→`J`: the CNN-vote gate (designed for ties)
   overrides a correct rank-1 on a stripe-shredded piece.
   *Preregisterable next step*: margin/confidence gates on all three
   rules (e.g. ld-swap only within 0.03 and only when the digit
   scores < 0.90; narrow-case never overriding a ≥0.99 match; CNN vote
   never firing when native rank-1 ≥ 0.73 and ink < 45 px). Full-suite
   3-run non-regression per the standing protocol; measured on dev
   material first. Expected lift: +1 item now (easy-0068 needs both
   a+b), possibly more where the same rules fire silently on
   currently-passing items — that is exactly what the 3-run gate is
   for.
2. **Wall-family routing absent from the shipping route.**
   dark-on-dark-0000's `006`→NEC and the `VIOLET` collapse happen
   under plain otsu; the measured S7c wall masks exist but
   `tools/reader_lex.py` never passes `--auto-family` (the S12 router
   is in the repo and measured). *Preregisterable next step*:
   `reader_lex --auto-family` variant, full 3-run gate + zero-regression
   on seeds 1/2 — the honest version of family use (router decides
   from pixels; no filename oracle). Expected lift: concentrated in
   the dark-on-dark wall items.
3. **Segmentation over-split / word-gap collapse.** easy-0023's M
   (→`lvl`) and dark-on-dark-0000's tokenisation. *Preregisterable
   next step*: an evidence-first merge table (adjacent pieces whose
   union-mask matches a wide glyph with margin) — dev-corpus measured;
   the historical morph-close/merge lanes stay closed, this is
   matcher-score-driven merging, not hope-driven masks.
4. **True glyph damage (truth not in the matcher's top-8).**
   snow-0003 (both positions), split-0006 (both), easy-0014 (3 of 4),
   easy-0001 (both), most of strippy-0005. Scoring cannot fix what
   the mask destroyed: e→r (closed counter opened), f→t (crossbar
   gone), B→m (stem lost), E→F (rung eroded), r→l (arm gone).
   *Candidate*: damage-tolerant template variants (pre-eroded
   templates) — requires a dev corpus and its own preregistration;
   the residue estimate below assumes it stays unsolved.

## Honest residue arithmetic

Of the 8 live items: 3 carry rule-induced components (easy-0068 ×2,
strippy-0005 ×1), 1 is wall-routing (dark-on-dark-0000), 2 are
over-split (easy-0023, partly dark-on-dark-0000), and 5 are pure
glyph damage (with overlap). If classes 1–3 were fully solved with
zero collateral, the ceiling is roughly hard 25→26–28, easy 75→76–78
— **still far from 98/95**, consistent with S55 §6's honest framing.
The glyph-damage class is the hard wall; no text-side rule reaches it.
