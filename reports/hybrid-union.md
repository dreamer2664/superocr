# Floor + Iris: the two-reader union, measured (pericles, 12 Oct evening)

Question on the floor: what can the classic reader and the vision reader
together reach, against the bar (98 easy / 95 hard)? Answer: measured today.

## Method
- Floor: `python3 baseline.py <image>` per item, current main, both suites
  (the exact official floor command; 200/200 items read, 0 errors).
- Iris: the completed full pass (200/200 live reads; see
  gemini/reports inside the chatcode workspace; the reader-side numbers here).
- Union = an item is "won" if EITHER reader returned the exact label.
  This is a **ceiling** for any two-reader ensemble with a perfect selector —
  it is not a shippable number (the selector problem stays open).

## What this says
1. **The vision reader dominates**: 131 items only Iris gets vs **2** only the
   floor gets. A hybrid buys +2 items over Iris-alone. An ensemble is not the
   road to the bar.
2. **The roads to the bar are**: (a) Iris's own residue — case twins and
   homoglyphs (see the classified miss list), where the floor's measured
   rulebook (barry's waves 2-5) already codifies the fix; (b) corrosion, which
   defeats BOTH readers (0/8 + 0/8) — the classifier wall is the one true wall
   left, and it deserves its own task, not incidental sweeps.
3. Even a perfect two-reader selector lands at 89/79 — **the bar is not
   reachable by combining what exists**; it needs at least one reader to
   improve on its own terms, or a third read strategy for the 32 "neither"
   items (corrosion 8 + easy residue 11 + the rest spread thin).
