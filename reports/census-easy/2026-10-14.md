## What the 25 misses actually are

- **The case lane owns 13 (52%).** Twelve are a single case decision on one
  token (dist floor→final 1→1 or 2→1): `pINNaCLe`≠`PINNaCLe`, `DriFT`≠`Drift`,
  `WREn`≠`WrEn`, `wALRUS`≠`wAlRUS`, `CIPHER`≠`CIPHEr`, `SUndiaL`≠`SUndIaL`,
  `jasPEr`≠`jaSPEr`, `SequOiA`≠`SequOIA`, `larCh`≠`larCH`, `GLaCieR`≠`GlaCieR`,
  `Indigo`≠`indigo`. One is a duplicate-token twin (`raven raVen` ≠
  `raven raven`). This is the reader-side `_case_of` lane, not lex re-casing
  (S22 records that as rejected) — the same lane S58 shows now owning 12 of
  the hard residue. **Across both suites, 25 misses are case-primary.**
- **Segmentation is 8, and it is small-scale.** Unlike hard's collapsed masks,
  6 of 8 are single-token corruptions inside otherwise exact lines: invented
  suffix token (`TARn NI` ≠ `TARN`), digit run (`Th131` ≠ `Tarn`), stray token
  (`… Ivory fT umBEr …`), one dropped-character pair (`Wolet` ≠ `Violet`),
  one affix split (`StA lACTUTT` ≠ `StAlACTITE`), one near-garble
  (`saMron` ≠ `saffron`). Only 2 read a whole phrase wrong
  (`eIIRF 7 anchor`, `DUNE zephyr StA lACTUTT`).
- **Glyph substitution is 4** — all under a surviving character structure:
  `BilTh`/`Birch` (lT↔rc ligature), `FFNmPI`/`FENNEL`, `meSa`+`nT`/`Mesa`+`07`,
  `D1rch`+`gq`/`birch`+`79`.
