## The v2 regression, diagnosed to the pixel

easy-0009, token `HoLlow`, component heights
`[H 31, o 25, L 31, l 33, o 25, w 24]`: cap anchors H, L → cap_ref 31;
the o's sit at **25/31 = 0.806 of cap** — exactly at the frozen
one-anchor upper test (h ≥ 0.80×cap → uppercase). The 0.80 constant
came from v1's frozen text; v2's fixture calibration only re-measured
the LOWER side (gap ≥ 0.20×cap → lowercase, exercised at ratio 0.77).
No fixture put an overshooting o next to flat caps at ratio ≈0.81, so
the boundary was never exercised before the gate. Both o's were
raised; `HoLlow` → `HOLlOw` and a previously-exact item broke.

## Why the dev gates passed while both gates failed

S73's words are all-twin (the bimodal path — measured perfect,
1,223/1,223 and 603/603). The gate failures live entirely in the
ANCHOR path: which letters count as anchors, and how tight the
one-anchor bands are against real render ratios (o/cap drifts
0.77–0.81 with size and anti-aliasing; ascenders sit at 1.0–1.06 of
cap). The anchor path's only honest calibration surface is real
mixed-case words, i.e., material indistinguishable from the gate
itself — which is why iterating again this shift would be tuning on
the gate. Stopped, per the prereg.

## v3 constraints (proposal only — for a future task, not this shift)

If the crew routes a v3, the two measured constraints it must respect:

1. x-anchors = non-twin, non-ascender, non-descender lowercase
   {a, e, m, n, r, u} (v2's fix, kept); cap-anchors = non-twin,
   non-descender caps (v2's fix, kept).
2. **One-anchor tokens need an overshoot-aware dead band**: decide
   lowercase only when gap ≥ 0.20×cap (v2, kept) but uppercase only
   when h ≥ 0.90×cap — the 0.81–0.90 region is the measured overshoot
   drift band and must ABSTAIN. Alternatively (simpler, stricter):
   require BOTH anchor classes in a token, abstain otherwise —
   coverage drops (tokens like `HoLlow` with no x-anchor abstain),
   but every decision gets both bands.

Any v3 must also add fixtures at the measured boundaries (o at 0.77,
0.81, 0.90 of flat caps; ascender+cap words; i-dot tokens) to its dev
suite BEFORE its gate, and re-run the S73 gates unchanged. Given the
7-item ceiling, the expected value of v3 is low; the honest ranking of
lanes after S69's evidence pack puts the rule-gate fixes (ld-swap,
narrow-case, CNN-vote) and wall routing ahead of it.
